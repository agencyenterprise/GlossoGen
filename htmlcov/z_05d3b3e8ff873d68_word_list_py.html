1"""The vocabulary signal_game draws word grids from, in the order ``word_count`` takes it. 

2 

3Concrete common English nouns, 3 to 8 letters of a to z, none of them a colour, shape or 

4fill name, so a word is never read as one of the other items. The players are never shown 

5this list: each sees only the words on its own grid, and the partner's after each round. 

6 

7The list is append-only: never reorder, change or remove a word. Boards take 

8``WORDS[:word_count]`` in order, so an edit changes the grids a recorded run replays when 

9it is resumed or forked, and the round panel recognises a square as a word only while 

10the word is in this list. A test pins the first 300 words. 

11"""
