56class CrateStart(str, Enum): 

57 """Where a round places the two crates. 

58 

59 ``CORNER`` puts both on the same square every round, which gives the 

60 operators a fixed anchor and makes every bay the same known distance away in 

61 every round. ``SHARED_RANDOM`` draws one square a round and puts both crates 

62 on it, so the geometry changes but both operators still know where the other 

63 started. ``SEPARATE_RANDOM`` draws a square per operator, so neither knows 

64 where the other's crate began. 

65 """
