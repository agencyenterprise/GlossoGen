1"""Projecting a probe-similarity artifact's groups into keyed observations. 

2 

3The replica-self and agent-pair artifacts differ in what identifies a group. One is 

4keyed by the agent, the other by the pair of agents the question was compared across. 

5Both carry one ``mean_similarity`` per group and both name the question and the 

6cutoff. So the projection is shared, and each group's own identifying fields come 

7along as keys without either metric listing them. 

8 

9Every scalar field on the group becomes a key. A list field (the agent ids of a pair, 

10the replica indices) is joined, because a key is a dimension cell and a cell holds 

11text. ``response_texts`` and ``cells`` are dropped: they are the evidence the number 

12was computed from, not an axis anything groups by.
