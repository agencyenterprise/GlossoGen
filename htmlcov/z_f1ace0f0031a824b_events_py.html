13class SpillwayCaseStarted(EventBase): 

14 """Emitted once at round start with the full ground-truth case. 

15 

16 ``current_time_hours`` and ``day_end_hours`` are shared with all three 

17 agents. ``start_level`` is private to the dam operator, ``inflow`` and 

18 ``forecast_conditions`` private to civil defense, and 

19 ``park_opens_at_hours`` / ``park_lockable`` / ``visitors`` private to the

22 """
