1"""The four MCP tools the spillway scenario exposes to its agents.

3``read_gauge`` and ``open_gates`` belong to the dam operator, ``notify_park`` 

4to the park ranger, and ``evacuate`` to civil defense. ``open_gates``, 

5``notify_park``, and ``evacuate`` record the agent's decision on the world
