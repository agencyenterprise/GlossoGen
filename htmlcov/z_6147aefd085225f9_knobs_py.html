10class SharedWorkspaceKnobs(BaseKnobs): 

11 """Factors of a layered crafting task solved by a team over one shared depot. 

12 

13 The task is a grid of recipes ``crafter_count`` columns wide and 

14 ``steps_per_agent`` layers deep: each column ends in one target, each recipe 

15 consumes the previous layer, and ``dag_cross_edge_density`` sets how often a 

16 recipe also consumes another column's previous output (at most 

17 ``dag_max_fan_in`` inputs from that layer). Work is ``crafter_count * 

18 steps_per_agent`` crafts and the critical path is ``steps_per_agent``. 

19 ``crafter_count`` may be 1, a pure chain. ``quantity_scale`` bounds each raw 

20 input quantity, ``dag_raw_material_count`` and ``dag_max_raw_inputs`` the raw 

21 items, and ``resource_slack_fraction`` adds that fraction of each raw item's 

22 exact need, rounded up. ``task_manifest`` replays certified tasks from a file 

23 instead of generating them. 

24 

25 ``pool_agent_count`` agents take part (``crafter_count`` when unset; 1 is the 

26 single-agent baseline). Every agent is briefed with every target and the 

27 depot; no target or column is assigned. ``recipe_holders`` deals each recipe 

28 to that many agents, the hands as even as possible; unset, every agent holds 

29 every recipe. Any agent may run any recipe whose exact command it has. 

30 

31 The team shares one pool of workspace actions: for each column, 

32 ``actions_per_witness_step`` times that column's share of the witness, 

33 rounded up, summed over the columns. ``team_token_limit`` caps the 

34 prompt plus completion tokens of every model response in a round, summed over 

35 the team; crossing it ends the round with ``team_tokens_exhausted``. 

36 ``uncraft_enabled`` lets ``act`` reverse a craft: ``uncraft <exact craft 

37 command>`` removes that recipe's output, returns its full inputs and costs one 

38 action. 

39 

40 ``virtual_clock`` orders agents by simulated hosted-API latency instead of by 

41 when the inference server answered: each request costs 

42 ``virtual_base_latency_s`` plus its output tokens at 

43 ``virtual_output_tokens_per_second``, and ``read_notifications`` timeouts count 

44 virtual seconds. ``craft_duration_s`` makes each accepted craft last that many 

45 virtual seconds: its inputs leave the depot when it starts, its output lands 

46 when it ends, and the crafter is busy in between while teammates act. Uncraft 

47 stays instantaneous. 

48 """
