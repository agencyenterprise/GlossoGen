21 """Configuration knobs for the spillway_release scenario. 

22 

23 ``round_time_budget_seconds`` is the per-round character budget on the 

24 ops channel: every character sent costs one simulated second and the 

25 round fails when the running total reaches the budget. 

26 ``channel_noise_level`` is the per-character drop probability on the 

27 ops channel; ``noise_replacement_mode`` selects what each dropped 

28 character becomes (``mask`` = visible erasure, ``random_letter`` = 

29 silent substitution). 

30 

31 ``gate_count`` is the number of identical spillway gates; each open 

32 gate sheds ``release_per_gate_per_hour`` percentage points of reservoir 

33 capacity per hour. ``max_level`` is the collapse threshold (the dam 

34 fails above it) and ``min_level`` is the shortage threshold (the round 

35 fails below it); the operator must land the end-of-round level inside 

36 ``[min_level, max_level]``. ``day_end_hours`` is the end of the 

37 operating horizon used for park-occupancy and release windows. 

38 

39 ``easy_round_numbers`` is the set of rounds forced to the ``hold`` 

40 archetype (calm warmups). Every other round draws its archetype from 

41 ``archetype_weights`` (positional, paired with 

42 :data:`ARCHETYPE_ORDER`: hold, time_it, keep_closed, evacuate). Each 

43 round is built from an independent per-round RNG so toggling one round 

44 never shifts another round's case under a fixed ``seed``.
