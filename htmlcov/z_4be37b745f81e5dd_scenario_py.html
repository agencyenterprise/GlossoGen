1"""Two depot operators who can affect each other only through a crate's temperature. 

2 

3Each operator moves a crate across its own floor to one of the loading bays. The 

4depot assigns each a different list of bays with one bay on both, so the bay 

5they can agree on is one neither can name alone. A bay is named by its square, 

6the same on every floor, or by its symbol or letter, with each floor's bays on 

7squares of their own. The depot readout 

8returns every crate's reading after every action, and how the crate is carried 

9decides what its reading does. Nothing in the task says the reading means 

10anything.
