1"""World simulation for the container_yard_stacking scenario. 

2 

3Tracks per-team yard-row state, the single ``move_container`` action, and 

4the running character count on each team's link channel. The world is 

5mutated synchronously by the one scenario tool: the crane operator submits 

6a source slot and a destination slot, ``record_move`` judges them against 

7the round's ``YardCase`` ground truth and the live row, and mutates the row 

8on accept. Every mutating method takes a ``team_id`` so two-team mode can 

9fork per-team state without changing call sites. 

10 

11A round relocates one or more target containers per team. Each relocation 

12is one "step": the team's crane operator moves the announced target to its 

13relative goal. Round success requires every step to be placed correctly and 

14the round budget not to have been exceeded. 

15 

16Heavy logic lives in dedicated sibling modules: :mod:`judging` (move 

17scoring), :mod:`outcome_reconstruction` (rebuilding outcomes from a JSONL 

18event log on resume), and :mod:`world_state` (the ``TeamState`` / 

19``YardOutcome`` types). 

20"""
