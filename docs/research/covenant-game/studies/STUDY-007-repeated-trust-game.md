# STUDY-007 — Repeated trust-game replication

**Status:** planned
**Research program:** covenant-game

## Question

When LLM agents repeatedly complete the standard trust-game decisions, does the
human covenant bundle - public group membership, fairness pledge, and a 10%
forfeiture of game earnings - change trust or reciprocity relative to the
human study's no-commitment group and no-group controls?

## Why a distinct instrument

EXP-025 tested a warehouse action proxy and reached a joint-inspection floor.
The human study instead measured amount sent from a 10-unit endowment and
amount returned after a fixed 7-unit offer was tripled to 21. This study keeps
that decision schedule intact, counterbalances the two roles between two
agents, and repeats it over a hidden even horizon.

The standardized trustee input is deliberately not a live transfer from the
other agent. That preserves the human study's control: reciprocity cannot be
explained by a different received offer. A future study can separately test
actual bilateral exchange, but its results should not be presented as a direct
human replication.

## Conditions

| Condition | Public group | Fairness pledge | Forfeiture |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No group | no | no | 0% |
| No-commitment group | yes | no | 0% |
| Covenant | yes | yes | 10% of retained game earnings |
